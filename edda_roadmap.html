Implementation Roadmap

Tier 1 High Impact — Build First

1

Track — Shelves, Status & Ratings

Smart shelves (Want to Read, Reading, Read, DNF), quarter-star ratings, reading status management, format tracking, Goodreads CSV import. Foundation for everything else.

2

Journal — Mood, Notes & Reviews

Mood/pace tags per book, progress-based notes, quote collection, post-read reviews with privacy toggle, custom tags, re-read support. Eliminates Notion dependency.

3

Goals — Challenges, Streaks & Wrap

Annual reading challenge with ahead/behind pace, reading streaks, achievement badges, daily goals, shareable Year-in-Review (Spotify Wrapped-style). Drives daily engagement.

4

Export — PKM Sync & Highlight Review

Markdown/JSON/CSV export with full metadata, Obsidian vault sync, Notion API sync, daily spaced-repetition highlight review. Replaces Readwise ($120/yr value).

Tier 2 Strong Differentiators — Build Next

5

Social — Activity Feed & Friends

Follow readers, activity feed, buddy reads, friend recommendations, per-book privacy controls, community content warnings.

6

Clubs — Book Club Hub

Create/join clubs, ranked-choice voting, reading schedules, chapter-gated spoiler-free discussions, AI discussion questions, shared progress dashboard.

7

Discovery — AI-Powered Recommendations

Mood-based search, "Because you liked X" recs, friend-powered suggestions, AI reading advisor, trending by genre.

Tier 3 Edda's Moat — Unique Differentiators

8

Enhanced AI Companion

"Story So Far" recaps for any book, AI character tracker, vocabulary builder with flashcards, comprehension Q&A, AI content warnings.

9

Audiobook Integration

Manual audiobook tracking, timestamp-to-text mapping, unified progress across reading & listening.

10

Anti-Lock-In Platform

Import from Goodreads/StoryGraph/Kindle, open API for third-party integrations, full data portability.

Key Research Insights

2026 State of Reading Report: "People I know personally" is now the #1 source of book discovery, surpassing algorithms. Readers want "AI that feels additive rather than intrusive."

Amazon Lock-In Trend: September 2025 lockdowns restricted My Notebook copying and introduced stronger Kindle encryption. Amazon is actively reducing user access to their own reading data.

Readwise Opportunity: Readwise exists because reading apps lock highlights and export in incompatible formats. An app that reads + exports natively eliminates the $120/year intermediary.

Format Convergence: 57% of readers now consume both audiobooks and ebooks. Smartphones are the #1 reading device. Seamless format switching is the top reader wish.

Gamification Works: Fable reports 300% YoY growth in reading streak engagement (average 29-day streaks). StoryGraph's quarter-star ratings and mood tags are the most cited reasons users switch from Goodreads.
